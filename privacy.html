Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

The short version: Sir Spatula does not collect any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud account.

This policy covers the Sir Spatula game for iPhone, iPad and Mac, made by Butler Makes LLC ("we" or "us").

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not send any information about you or how you play to us or to anyone else. We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, and we never sell or share data, because we have none.

What the game saves on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves these on your device only:

  • Your coins, Pantry upgrades, armors and wins in each kitchen
  • The dishes you have cooked (the Cookbook)
  • A run you saved to continue later
  • Your settings, such as music, sound effects and vibration

Deleting the game deletes this information.

iCloud backup

If you are signed in to iCloud, the game backs up your coins and Pantry upgrades to your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud storage, so they come back if you get a new device. This backup is stored by Apple in your account. We cannot see it or access it. You can turn it off in your device's Settings under your name, then iCloud.

Purchases

Coin packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never receive your name, Apple Account, email address or payment details. The game only learns that a coin pack was bought so it can add the coins. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing your recipe card

At the end of a run you can tap Share to send your recipe card picture. This uses your device's own share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it, and it goes only where you send it.

Children

Sir Spatula is suitable for players of many ages. Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
