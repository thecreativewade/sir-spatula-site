Cook your way through a veggie rebellion.

For iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Sir Spatula is a tiny kitchen knight facing an army of rogue vegetables. Survive six courses, from Appetizer to Dessert, pick a new tool or ingredient every time you level up, and combine them into dishes like Inferno Flip and Volcano Ramen.

Every run ends with a recipe card you can share.

  • Three kitchens: the Diner, the Ramen Shop and the Bakery
  • Eighteen bosses, from Carrot Commander to Queen Layer Cake
  • Pantry upgrades and armors, all earned by playing
  • No ads, no energy timers, no tracking
