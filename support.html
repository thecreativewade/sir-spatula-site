Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? We read every email.

Email us

Write to joshua@butlermakes.com. It helps to tell us which device you play on (iPhone, iPad or Mac) and what happened.

I bought coins but they didn't show up

Close the game completely and open it again. The game checks with the App Store each time it starts and adds any coins that were paid for but not delivered. If they still don't appear, email us and include the date of the purchase.

Will I lose my coins on a new device?

Not if you're signed in to iCloud. Your coins and Pantry upgrades are backed up to your iCloud account and come back when you install the game on a new iPhone or iPad signed in to the same account.

Can I stop in the middle of a run?

Yes. Pause and choose Save and quit, or just leave the game. Next time, tap Continue on the title screen to pick up exactly where you were. Saved runs stay on the device where you saved them.

How do I play on a Mac?

Move with the WASD or arrow keys, or click and drag. Press Esc to pause.

Is everything for sale?

No. Every upgrade, armor and knight can be earned by playing. Coin packs only get you there faster. There are no ads, no energy timers and no random prize packs.

How do I turn down the music or sound?

Tap the speaker button on the title screen, or open the pause menu during a run.
